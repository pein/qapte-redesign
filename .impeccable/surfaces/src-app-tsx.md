# Surface brief — Home

Mode: Persuade

## Audience & job

Technology and business leaders at financial-services and insurance organizations, evaluating whether QUAPT can take them from AI uncertainty to governed production systems. They need to judge rigor, regulatory fluency, and delivery credibility quickly, then take the readiness-assessment action. Secondary audience: design and engineering peers judging the craft of this concept build.

## Action & proof

Primary action: start the AI readiness self-check (working, client-side, clearly labelled illustrative) with a path to the full assessment. Secondary: explore method, systems, ICXBee, and perspectives.

Proof on hand: real public metrics, real client names, real service descriptions, real use cases, real article titles, and QUAPT's own ICXBee product. No fabricated testimonials, clients, benchmarks, or case studies. All demo traces, charts, and assessment outputs are synthetic and labelled.

## Constraints

- React + Vite + TypeScript, Tailwind, ui-sdk.dev components/behaviors.
- Front-end showcase only: no backend, no real submissions.
- Enterprise-credible but design-led; clean, minimalist, ambitious.
- Must not resemble the incumbent's stock-photo hero, blue gradient, rounded-card system, or bracket eyebrows. The old look is anti-reference.
- Accessible: semantic structure, keyboard operation, visible focus, reduced motion, AA contrast.
- Responsive from 390px to 1600px.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is QUAPT's system card: AI presented as an assessed, evidenced, overseen system. It refuses the category's stock-photo hero, blue gradient, and equal-card grid; proof is shown as a document and a working check, not claimed in a headline.

OWN-WORLD: Archive paper, near-black ink, hairline rules, square edges, safety-orange approval marks, rare verdigris green for verified status. Archivo variable sets the editorial scale; Martian Mono is reserved for measurements, timestamps, hashes, and document metadata. Ledger tables, stamps, clause references, and technical diagrams replace cards and icons.

STORY: The visitor sees an agent decision trace with human oversight, understands QUAPT's readiness-to-autonomy method, sees the regulated use cases and real outcomes, tries the readiness check, and books the full assessment.

FIRST VIEWPORT: 12-column dossier cover. Left: "AI that survives the audit." at monumental scale, a short position statement, and the readiness action. Right: a dark System Card 001 panel animating a synthetic claims-triage trace that ends in human approval. A ruled ledger beneath carries the real metrics and client names as document data, not stat blocks. Masthead nav is a numbered index with a live status line.

FORM: Grounded direction 7 of 7 — the assurance dossier / model card. seed:c1b7635eca2c00f3.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
